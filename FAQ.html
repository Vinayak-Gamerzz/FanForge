FANFORGE FAQ

Got questions?

We've got answers

Everything you probably wanted to know beofre starting your FanForge Ship

What is FanForge ?

It is a YSWS where you turn a fandom you love into a creative web project of your own

Who can Participate ?

Anyone who is in the age group 13-18 can participate.

What counts as a fandom?

Pretty much anything you genuinely enjoy games, movies, shows, books, anime, comics, characters, or even a fictional world you made yourself.

What is a FanForge “Ship”?

Your Ship is the project you build and submit to FanForge. Think of it as your finished creation that you're sending into the Forge.

What can I build?

You can build a website around a fandom you genuinely enjoy. It could be a character page, gallery, collection, interactive experience, or something completely your own.

Do I need to use Three.js ?

Nope! Three.js and 3D characters are completely optional. They're one possible way to make your project interactive, not a requirement.

Do I need to use JavaScript ?

Not necessarily. You can build your project with HTML and CSS, and use JavaScript when your idea needs it.

Can I use existing assets ?

Yes, as long as you're allowed to use them. Check the license for anything you use and provide credit when required.

How big does my project need to be ?

There isn't a required number of pages. Focus on making something complete and enjoyable rather than making the biggest website possible.

How do I submit my project?

Once your Ship is ready, submit it through the official FanForge submission form with all the required project details.

What should I check before submitting?

Make sure your website works, your links are working, your required features are included, and your project follows the FanForge requirements.

Can I submit an unfinished project?

Your project should be finished enough for someone else to explore and use. Test everything before submitting.

What happens after I submit?

Your project will be reviewed against the FanForge requirements. If it meets the criteria, it can be approved for the YSWS reward.

Can I submit more than once?

Follow the submission limits and instructions provided by FanForge before submitting another Ship.

I don't know what to build. Where do I start?

Start with a fandom you already know and love. Pick a character, world, story, or idea and ask yourself: "What would I want to explore?"

I'm new to coding. Can I still participate?

Absolutely. You don't need to know everything before starting. Learn as you build, experiment, and keep improving your Ship.

My idea doesn't fit perfectly. Can I still build it?

If your idea is based around a fandom and follows the FanForge requirements, go for it. There's no single "correct" type of Ship.

What if I get stuck while building?

Break the problem into smaller pieces, look through documentation or examples, and experiment. You can also ask the community for help.

Where can I find the important requirements?

Check the Requirements and Rules pages before submitting. They'll give you the details you need to make sure your Ship is eligible.

What rewards can I earn?

FanForge rewards are based on the Ship you build and whether it meets the requirements. Check the Requirements page for the current reward criteria.

Does every Ship get a reward?

A Ship needs to meet the FanForge requirements before it can qualify for a reward. Submitting a project alone does not automatically guarantee one.

How do I qualify for a reward?

Build a genuine fandom-based project, follow the rules, complete the required features, and submit your Ship correctly for review.

What if my Ship doesn't meet the requirements?

If your Ship doesn't meet the requirements, it may not qualify for a reward. Make sure to check the requirements before submitting.

When will I receive my reward?

Rewards are processed after your Ship has been reviewed and approved. Any specific delivery details will be provided with the reward information.
