Rules of the Forge

Build what you wantttt:D

FanForge is meant to give you a place to turn something you love into something other people can experience. Rules will keep forge fair, safe and cool for everyone

01 - Build something you actually like

FanForge is about fandoms, so start with something you genuinely enjoy watching or reading

Pick a character, game, movie, show, book, anime, or anything else you're a fan of then build something around it

If you dont want to explore your own website, rethink of that idea then

02 - Make it your own

Getting inspired by other website is completely fine. Following tutorials is fine too

But your ifnal project should have your own ideas, design, and work in it

Don't take someone else's website and change a few things beofre submitting it.

03 - Use atlesat 15 HTML/CSS features

Every FanForge project needs to use at least 15 different HTML/CSS features

Don't add random elements just to reach the number

Use them because they actually help build your website

15 meaningful > 15 random ones

04 - Make sure it works

Before submitting, tkae a minute to actually use your website

Click the buttons

Open the links

Check the pages

Try the interactive parts

If something is broken, fix it beofre you ship or it'll get rejected |Y-Y|

05 - Using outside assets is okay

You don't have to create every single thing yourself

Images, fonts, icons, models, libraries, and other resources can be used when their licenses allow it

06 - Don't claim work you didn't do

Your submission should represent what you actually built

Don't submit someone else's project as your own, and don't pretend you made something that you didn't

The point is to build

07 - You don't need to build something massive

Your website doesn't need dozens of pages or thousands of lines of code

A small project with a good idea and a finished experience is better than a huge project tbh cause toship first you need to start and then finish it

Focus on making something complete

08 - There is no "right" type of project

You could make a character showcase

A gallery

A timeline

An interactive story

A collection

Or something we haven't thought of yet

If it fits FanForge, go for it

That's it

Pick something you love

Build something with it

Make it yours

Ship it

Welcome to the Forge!
